# Japanese deinflection: rule groups and their sources

The Japanese deinflector in `crates/core/src/deinflection/japanese/` undoes inflections by replacing kana endings, chaining rules so that stacked inflections are undone one at a time. Every rule group is drawn from the source cited here and in a doc comment above its table. Rules that no source supports are left out and listed under [Unsourced](#unsourced).

No GPL or GPL-derived deinflector (Yomitan, Yomichan, 10ten Reader, rikaichan, rikaikun, Nazeka, or the crates and packages derived from them) and no JMdictDB conjugation table was read or used. The test fixture `fixtures/kamiya-cases.json` is generated by `kamiya-codec` (Unlicense), which serves only as a test oracle and is not a source for any rule.

## Word classes

Results carry the six classes that Yomitan-format dictionaries put in their rules column: `v1` (ichidan), `v5` (godan), `vk` (来る), `vs` (する and its compounds), `vz` (ずる verbs) and `adj-i`. While a chain is being undone, rules also pass through intermediate states of our own: the continuative stem, the irrealis stem, the two euphonic stems (before た/て and before だ/で), and the adjective stem. Rules that turn a stem back into a dictionary form undo no inflection of their own; the suffix rule that produced the stem names the inflection.

When two chains reach the same term with the same inflections, their classes are merged into one result.

## Unsourced

These were left out because none of the sources above states them:

- The negative imperative な (as in するな). WP-main shows it only inside example sentences, without describing the form.
- The representative かったり of i-adjectives in general. Only なかったり, from the paradigm of ない in WKT-verbs, is included.
- Colloquial contractions beyond those listed: なきゃ, なくちゃ, ちゃ and じゃ for ては and では, and ば-contractions such as 書きゃ. The research report points to IPADIC `Verb.csv` for the last; it has not been checked yet.
- Kansai and other dialect forms, such as へん, and the classical nidan classes.

## Sourced but not yet implemented

- The negative volitional まい (Wiktionary entry まい).
- たがる, やすい, にくい and the other continuative suffixes in WP-renyou beyond those above.
- The subsidiaries てある, てみる, ていく and てく, てくる, ておる and とる (WP-renyou `#Subsidiaries`).
- The contraction of ら-row negatives to ん, as in 分かんない (WP-mizen `#Negative:_Conjugation_table`).
- ゆく as a reading of 行く, with its euphonic stem ゆっ (WP-renyou `#Gerund:_Conjugation_table`).

## Points of uncertainty

- ちゃう and ちまう are treated as godan verbs, like しまう. WKT-chau gives no conjugation class; the godan forms in WP-renyou's examples (ちゃった, ちまって, じゃえ) are consistent with it.
- Group B する compounds that behave like godan verbs (愛さない, 愛せる) trace back to the godan form (愛す, `v5`), not to 愛する (`vs`). A dictionary that lists only 愛する will not match them.
- ない is traced back to ある only when it is the whole word, and only in kana.
- Rules work on kana endings, so the irregular forms of 来る are listed in both kana and kanji (来ない, こない), and the honorific verbs in the spellings named by the sources.
